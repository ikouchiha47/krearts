# STYLE: Pop Art Comic

## Overview
Bold graphic style inspired by Roy Lichtenstein and the Pop Art movement. Takes comic book aesthetics and amplifies them to fine art scale. Characterized by exaggerated Ben-Day dots, flat primary colors, thick outlines, and dramatic emotional expressions.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Line Work**: Thick, bold black outlines, uniform weight
- **Anatomy**: Simplified, graphic, poster-like
- **Expressions**: Exaggerated emotional faces
- **Panel Density**: Often single panel/image focus (1-4 large panels)
- **Colors**: Flat primary colors (red, yellow, blue) with black
- **Composition**: Cropped, dramatic, poster-like presentation

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: Single dramatic panel or 2-4 large panels
- **Focus**: One powerful image over sequential narrative
- **Cropping**: Extreme close-ups, cut-off compositions
- **Text Integration**: Words as visual elements
- **Scale**: Large format, poster-like presentation

## Character Design References
- Exaggerated facial expressions
- Bold, simplified features
- Dramatic eye makeup and styling
- 1960s fashion and hair
- Emotional intensity in every face

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Extreme close-ups on faces
- Cropped at unusual points
- Text bubbles as compositional elements
- Single powerful image
- Poster-like presentation

## Color Palette References
- **Primary**: Pure red (#FF0000), Yellow (#FFFF00), Blue (#0000FF)
- **Secondary**: Limited, flat application
- **Black**: Bold outlines and shadows
- **White**: Background and highlights
- **Dots**: Single color dots on contrasting background

## 0. Scope & Intent
Pop Art comic style elevating comic book aesthetics to fine art. Inspired by Roy Lichtenstein's iconic works. Designed for bold, graphic impact with emotional intensity and commercial art sensibility.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
Large-scale comic art with visible Ben-Day dots, flat primary colors, and dramatic emotional content. Art as commentary on commercial culture.

### Mood & Tone
- Dramatic
- Ironic
- Bold
- Commercial

### Panel Density
1-4 panels per page, often single powerful image

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Thick, uniform black outlines
- Consistent weight throughout
- Bold, confident strokes
- No variation or tapering

### Shading
- Large visible Ben-Day dots
- Consistent dot size and spacing
- Single color dots on contrasting ground
- No gradients or blending

### Texture
- Ben-Day dot patterns only
- Flat color fields
- No painterly effects
- Print-inspired aesthetic

### Avoid
- Subtle gradients
- Realistic rendering
- Varied line weights
- Complex color mixing
- Painterly textures

## 3. Canonical Panel Examples

### Establishing
- Rarely used (focus on close-ups)
- If used, simplified graphic shapes
- Bold color blocks
- Minimal detail

### Character
- Extreme close-ups on faces
- Dramatic emotional expressions
- Cropped at unusual points
- Text integrated into composition

### Action
- Single explosive moment
- Bold sound effect as art
- Flat color explosion
- Graphic simplification

### Detail
- Eyes with dramatic makeup
- Lips as bold graphic element
- Hair as pattern
- Tears as graphic shapes

## 6. Color System

- **Primary Only**: Red, Yellow, Blue

- **No Mixing**: Colors remain pure and flat

## 7. Prompt Specification (Authoritative)

### Base Prompt
```
Pop art comic illustration in Roy Lichtenstein style.
Large visible Ben-Day dot patterns throughout.
Flat primary colors only: red, yellow, blue, black, white.
Thick uniform black outlines with no variation.

Single dramatic image or minimal panel composition.
Extreme close-up cropping on emotional faces.
Text bubbles and sound effects as visual art elements.
Poster-like presentation with bold graphic impact.

Characters with exaggerated emotional expressions.
1960s styling: dramatic eye makeup, bold lips, period hair.
Ben-Day dot shading on skin and all surfaces.
Melodramatic content and frozen dramatic moments.

No gradients, no subtle shading, no realistic rendering.
Flat color fields with hard edges.
Commercial art aesthetic elevated to fine art.
Print-inspired with visible dot patterns.

Lichtenstein influence throughout.
Bold, ironic, dramatic.
```

### Negative Prompt
```
subtle gradients, realistic rendering, varied line weights,
complex color mixing, painterly textures, soft shading,
modern styling, photorealistic, muted colors,
small dots, invisible halftone, smooth transitions,
multiple panels, sequential narrative, action sequences
```

## 8. Sound Effects & Onomatopoeia

### Iconic Sound Effects (As Art)
- `WHAAM!` - The famous Lichtenstein
- `BLAM!` - Explosive impact
- `POW!` - Classic punch
- `VAROOM!` - Engine/speed
- `TAKKA TAKKA` - Gunfire

### Emotional Text (Thought Bubbles)
- `I DON'T CARE! I'D RATHER SINK...`
- `THAT'S THE WAY IT SHOULD HAVE BEGUN!`
- `OH, JEFF... I LOVE YOU, TOO... BUT...`
- `M-MAYBE HE BECAME ILL...`

### Text Style Characteristics
- ALL CAPS always
- Dramatic punctuation (! and ...)
- Melodramatic content
- Period-appropriate dialogue
- Text as visual composition

### Lettering Style
- HUGE, bold, primary colors for effects
- Perfect oval speech bubbles with thick borders
- Cloud-shaped thought bubbles
- Bold hand-lettered style font
- Text IS the art, not addition
