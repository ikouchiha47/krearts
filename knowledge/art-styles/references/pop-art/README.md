# Pop Art Comic Reference Images

Add 2-3 reference images here to influence the pop art comic style.

## Recommended Images:
- Ben-Day dot patterns
- Bold flat colors
- Exaggerated expressions
- Thought bubbles and speech balloons
- Lichtenstein-style compositions

## Supported Formats:
- JPG/JPEG
- PNG
- WebP

## Usage:
These images will be automatically loaded when generating pages with
the "pop-art" art style.
