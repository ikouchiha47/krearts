# STYLE: Anime-Manga

## Overview
Japanese animation and comic art style characterized by expressive characters, dynamic action, and distinctive visual storytelling conventions. Encompasses a wide range from shonen action to shojo romance, unified by core aesthetic principles.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Eyes**: Large, expressive eyes with detailed highlights and reflections
- **Line Work**: Clean, confident lines with variable weight for emphasis
- **Anatomy**: Stylized proportions (large heads, slim bodies, long limbs)
- **Expressions**: Exaggerated emotional reactions (sweat drops, anger veins, sparkles)
- **Panel Density**: 4-6 panels per page, generous white space
- **Colors**: Clean, cel-shaded with gradient highlights
- **Composition**: Character-focused with minimal but impactful backgrounds

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: 4-6 panels with varied sizes
- **Dialogue Heavy**: 5-7 smaller panels
- **Action Scenes**: 2-4 large dynamic panels
- **Emotional Moments**: Single large panel or full page
- **Reading Direction**: Right-to-left (traditional manga)

## Character Design References
- Large expressive eyes with multiple highlight points
- Simplified nose (often just a line or small shape)
- Varied hair styles with distinct silhouettes
- Exaggerated body language and poses
- Costume designs that reflect personality

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Diagonal panel borders for tension
- Overlapping panels for simultaneous action
- Borderless panels for emotional moments
- Speed lines filling entire backgrounds
- Character breaking panel borders

## Color Palette References
- **Shonen Action**: Bold primaries, orange/blue contrast
- **Shojo Romance**: Soft pastels, pinks, flower motifs
- **Seinen Dark**: Muted tones, high contrast shadows
- **Isekai Fantasy**: Vibrant saturated colors, magical glows

## 0. Scope & Intent
Japanese animation and comic art style characterized by expressive characters, dynamic action, and distinctive visual storytelling conventions. Encompasses a wide range from shonen action to shojo romance, unified by core aesthetic principles. Designed for both print manga and animated adaptation.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
Clean cel-shaded illustration with expressive character design, dynamic compositions, and distinctive visual conventions for emotion and action.

### Mood & Tone
- Expressive
- Dynamic
- Character-focused
- Emotionally heightened

### Panel Density
4-6 panels per page (generous white space, varied sizes)

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Clean, confident contours
- Variable weight for emphasis
- Smooth curves for hair and fabric
- Sharp lines for action moments

### Shading
- Cel-shaded with hard edges
- Gradient highlights on hair/eyes
- Minimal cross-hatching
- Screen tones for texture

### Texture
- Simplified material rendering
- Hair rendered as flowing masses
- Fabric with dynamic folds
- Minimal environmental detail

### Avoid
- Realistic proportions
- Muted color palettes
- Dense cross-hatching
- Photorealistic rendering
- Static compositions

## 3. Canonical Panel Examples

### Establishing
- Character-focused with minimal background
- Dramatic sky or abstract patterns
- Speed lines for atmosphere
- Symbolic imagery (cherry blossoms, stars)

### Character
- Large expressive eyes with multiple highlights
- Simplified nose (line or small shape)
- Exaggerated hair silhouettes
- Dynamic poses even in static moments

### Action
- Extreme foreshortening
- Speed lines radiating from impact
- Panel borders broken by characters
- Energy auras and effects

### Detail
- Eye close-ups with reflections
- Hands in dramatic gestures
- Costume details reflecting personality
- Symbolic objects (weapons, accessories)

## 4. Character Design

### Anatomy
- Stylized proportions (large heads, slim bodies)
- Long limbs for elegance
- Exaggerated poses and gestures
- Age indicated by eye size and height

### Faces
- Large expressive eyes with detailed highlights
- Simplified nose and mouth
- Hair as defining feature
- Emotion symbols (sweat drops, anger veins)

### Clothing
- Distinctive silhouettes per character
- School uniforms, fantasy armor, casual wear
- Flowing fabric in motion
- Accessories as character markers

### Pose Language
- Exaggerated body language
- Dynamic even when standing
- Hands express emotion
- Chibi mode for comedy

## 5. Layout & Composition

- 4-6 panels with varied sizes
- Diagonal borders for tension
- Overlapping panels for simultaneous action
- Borderless panels for emotional moments
- Characters breaking panel borders
- Right-to-left reading (traditional manga)
- Full-page spreads for impact

## 6. Color System

### Shonen Action
- Bold primaries
- Orange/blue contrast
- High saturation
- Energy effect colors

### Shojo Romance

- Pinks and lavenders

- Magical glows

- Ethereal lighting

## 7. Prompt Specification (Authoritative)

### Base Prompt
```
Japanese anime manga art style comic illustration composed as a multi-panel page.
Clean cel-shaded coloring with hard-edged shadows and gradient highlights.
Confident linework with variable weight for emphasis and expression.
No realistic proportions, no muted palettes.

Panel composition favors character-focused framing with dynamic angles.
Diagonal panel borders create tension and energy. Characters break panel
borders for dramatic effect. Speed lines fill backgrounds during action.
Borderless panels for emotional moments.

Characters drawn with stylized anime proportions: large expressive heads,
slim bodies, long elegant limbs. Faces feature large detailed eyes with
multiple highlight points, simplified noses, and expressive mouths.
Hair rendered as flowing masses with distinct silhouettes.

Expressions are exaggerated and emotionally heightened. Visual symbols
communicate emotion: sweat drops for nervousness, anger veins for fury,
sparkles for beauty or magic, dark aura for menace.

Action scenes use extreme foreshortening, dense speed lines radiating
from impact points, energy auras, and dynamic poses. Motion is stylized
and exaggerated, not realistic.

Clean, vibrant color palette appropriate to genre. Cel-shaded rendering
with gradient highlights on hair and eyes. Professional manga illustration
aesthetic with anime adaptation sensibility.
```

### Negative Prompt
```
realistic proportions, photorealistic rendering, muted earth tones,
dense cross-hatching, Western comic style, static poses, small eyes,
detailed realistic noses, gritty textures, desaturated colors,
ligne claire, European BD style, American superhero anatomy
```

## 8. Sound Effects & Onomatopoeia

### Impact/Action Sounds
- `ドン` (DON) - Dramatic impact/appearance
- `バン` (BAN) - Gunshot/explosion
- `ガッ` (GA) - Sudden grab/catch
- `ドカ` (DOKA) - Heavy punch
- `バキ` (BAKI) - Breaking/cracking

### Motion Sounds
- `シュッ` (SHU) - Swift movement
- `ダッ` (DA) - Running start
- `ビュン` (BYUN) - Fast pass-by
- `ヒュー` (HYUU) - Falling/flying

### Emotional/Reaction Sounds
- `ドキドキ` (DOKIDOKI) - Heartbeat (excitement/love)
- `ガーン` (GAAN) - Shock/dismay
- `キラキラ` (KIRAKIRA) - Sparkling
- `シーン` (SHIIN) - Silence/awkward pause
- `ゴゴゴ` (GOGOGO) - Menacing aura

### Ambient Sounds
- `ザー` (ZAA) - Rain
- `ゴォォ` (GOOO) - Wind/roar
- `パチパチ` (PACHIPACHI) - Crackling fire

### English Equivalents
- Impacts: `BAM` `POW` `WHAM` `CRACK`
- Motion: `WHOOSH` `ZOOM` `DASH`
- Emotions: `THUMP THUMP` `SPARKLE` `SHOCK`
- Ambient: `RUSTLE` `SPLASH` `CRACKLE`

### Lettering Style
- Sound effects integrated into art
- Hand-drawn style lettering
- Size varies dramatically with intensity
- Round bubbles for normal speech
- Spiky bubbles for shouting
- Cloud bubbles for thoughts
- Effects follow action direction
