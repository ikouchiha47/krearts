# STYLE: American Comic (Superhero)

## Overview
Classic American superhero comic book style epitomized by Marvel and DC comics. Bold linework, dynamic action poses, vibrant primary colors, and muscular character designs. The foundation of Western comic art.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Line Work**: Bold, confident outlines with variable weight
- **Anatomy**: Heroic proportions (broad shoulders, narrow waist)
- **Expressions**: Dynamic, expressive masked and unmasked faces
- **Panel Density**: 5-7 panels, varied sizes for pacing
- **Colors**: Vibrant primary colors (red, blue, yellow)
- **Composition**: Dramatic angles, characters breaking panel borders

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: 5-7 panels with varied sizes
- **Dialogue Heavy**: Tighter 6-8 panel grids
- **Action Pages**: 3-4 large dynamic panels
- **Splash Pages**: Full page hero shots, common
- **Double Spreads**: Major battles and reveals

## Character Design References
- Heroic proportions (8-9 heads tall)
- Muscular definition visible through costumes
- Iconic costume designs with symbols
- Dynamic capes and flowing elements
- Expressive masked faces

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Characters breaking panel borders
- Extreme foreshortening (fist toward viewer)
- Diagonal compositions for action
- Upward angles for heroic shots
- Downward angles for menace

## Color Palette References
- **Heroes**: Primary colors (Superman blue/red, Spider-Man red/blue)
- **Villains**: Greens, purples, darker tones
- **Energy**: Bright yellows, electric blues
- **Backgrounds**: Simplified, focus on characters
- **Shadows**: Deep blacks, minimal grey

## 0. Scope & Intent
Classic American superhero comic book style representing the foundation of Western comic art. Bold, dynamic, and colorful. Designed for action-packed superhero narratives with iconic character designs and dramatic compositions.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
Bold linework, heroic proportions, vibrant primary colors, and dynamic action. The definitive superhero comic aesthetic.

### Mood & Tone
- Heroic
- Dynamic
- Bold
- Action-packed

### Panel Density
5-7 panels per page, with frequent splash pages for dramatic moments

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Bold, confident outlines
- Variable weight for depth
- Heavy blacks for drama
- Clean, professional inking

### Shading
- Deep blacks for shadows
- Minimal grey tones
- Dramatic lighting
- Form-defining shadows

### Texture
- Minimal texture effects
- Focus on clean rendering
- Costume material differentiation
- Energy effects with glow

### Avoid
- Sketchy, loose lines
- Muted color palettes
- Realistic proportions
- Static compositions
- Minimal action

## 8. Sound Effects & Onomatopoeia

### Impact Sounds
- `POW` - Classic punch
- `WHAM` - Heavy impact
- `BAM` - Strike
- `CRACK` - Breaking
- `SMASH` - Destruction
- `THWACK` - Hit

### Energy/Power Sounds
- `ZAP` - Energy blast
- `FWOOSH` - Fire/energy
- `CRACKLE` - Electricity
- `BOOM` - Explosion
- `KABOOM` - Big explosion

### Motion Sounds
- `WHOOSH` - Fast movement
- `ZOOM` - Speed
- `SWOOSH` - Swinging/flying
- `THWIP` - Web-shooting

### Combat Sounds
- `CLANG` - Metal on metal
- `SLASH` - Cutting
- `THUD` - Body hit
- `CRASH` - Through wall/window

### Lettering Style
- Large, bold, colorful effects
- Colors match action (red for fire, blue for ice)
- Traditional oval speech bubbles
- Spiky bubbles for shouting
- Yellow caption boxes for narration
- Sound effects ARE part of composition

## 9. Visual Effect Conventions

- **Speed Lines**: Radiating from action
- **Impact Stars**: Starburst at hit points
- **Energy Auras**: Glowing around powered characters
- **Motion Blur**: Streaking for fast movement
- **Kirby Krackle**: Dots for cosmic energy
- **Panel Breaking**: Characters extending beyond borders
