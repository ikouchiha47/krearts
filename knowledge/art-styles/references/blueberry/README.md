# STYLE: Blueberry European Bande Dessinée

## Overview
Realistic European bande dessinée (BD) style pioneered by Jean Giraud (Moebius) in the Blueberry Western comic series. Designed for full-color print album format with meticulous detail and naturalistic rendering.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Line Work**: Clean ligne claire with naturalistic shading and cross-hatching
- **Anatomy**: Realistic proportions with grounded, believable bodies
- **Expressions**: Subtle psychological expressions, weathered faces
- **Panel Density**: 8-9 panels per page (classic 3x3 grid)
- **Colors**: Muted, naturalistic earth tone palette
- **Composition**: Wide panoramic landscapes with atmospheric depth

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: 8-9 panels in dense grid format
- **Dialogue Heavy**: 9-panel grid with smaller frames
- **Action Scenes**: Sequential movement panels, clear spatial continuity
- **Establishing Shots**: Wide horizontal panoramas
- **Reading Direction**: Left-to-right (Western standard)

## Character Design References
- Realistic proportions with age and wear visible
- Expressive, weathered faces with strong noses
- Detailed eyes conveying psychological depth
- Period-accurate Western attire and equipment
- Natural, observational pose language

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Dense 9-panel grids for storytelling
- Wide panoramic establishing shots
- Stable horizons with clear geography
- Horizontal emphasis for landscape scale
- Grounded, observational camera angles

## Color Palette References
- **Earth Tones**: Ochre, sienna, dusty browns
- **Sky**: Muted blues, sunset oranges and pinks
- **Vegetation**: Sage greens, dried grass yellows
- **Shadows**: Cool purples and blues
- **Skin**: Warm, sun-weathered variations

## 0. Scope & Intent
Realistic European bande dessinée (BD) style pioneered by Jean Giraud (Moebius) in the Blueberry Western comic series. Designed for full-color print album format with meticulous detail and naturalistic rendering.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
Clean ligne claire with naturalistic shading, emphasizing landscape scale, character psychology, and historical authenticity.

### Mood & Tone
- Contemplative
- Grounded
- Cinematic
- Historically authentic

### Panel Density
8-9 panels per page (classic 3x3 grid for dense storytelling)

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Clean, controlled contours
- Fine lines with expressive variation
- Variable weight for depth
- Precise but not sterile

### Shading
- Cross-hatching and ink wash
- Naturalistic light and shadow
- Subtle gradients allowed
- Atmospheric perspective

### Texture
- Detailed environmental textures
- Fabric folds, leather, dust
- Weathered surfaces
- Natural materials

### Avoid
- Speed lines
- Exaggerated motion effects
- Manga-style expressions
- Flat color fills
- Digital smoothness

## 3. Canonical Panel Examples

### Establishing
- Wide panoramic landscapes
- Horizontal panels for geography
- Desert mesas, frontier towns
- Atmospheric depth

### Character
- Mid-distance framing
- Psychological expressions
- Period-accurate costume detail
- Grounded camera angles

### Action
- Sequential movement (not distortion)
- Body positioning tells story
- Dust clouds, realistic motion
- Clear spatial continuity

### Detail
- Hands, eyes, equipment
- Historical accuracy
- Weathered props
- Environmental storytelling

## 4. Character Design

## 7. Prompt Specification (Authoritative)

### Base Prompt
```
Realistic European bande dessinée comic illustration composed as a multi-panel page.
Clean, controlled linework with fine contours and expressive strokes.
Naturalistic inking with cross-hatching and ink wash shading.
No speed lines, no exaggerated motion effects.

Panel composition favors wide panoramic establishing shots and mid-distance
character framing. Horizontal panels emphasize landscape scale and geography.
Camera angles are grounded and observational, with stable horizons and
clear spatial continuity. Action is depicted through body positioning
and sequential movement, not distortion.

Characters drawn with realistic anatomy and proportional bodies.
Faces are expressive and weathered, with strong noses, defined cheekbones,
and detailed eyes. Facial expressions are subtle and psychologically grounded.
Hands, eyes, and posture carry emotional weight.

Clothing and props reflect historical accuracy: period western attire,
hats, coats, boots, revolvers, saddles, and worn equipment.
Materials show age, dust, and wear without exaggeration.

Environments are expansive and natural: deserts, plains, rocky terrain,
frontier towns, wooden structures, open skies.
Landscapes are rendered with depth and atmospheric perspective.

Muted, naturalistic color palette with earth tones.
Printed European comic album aesthetic.
Clean ligne claire influence with naturalistic rendering.
```

### Negative Prompt
```
speed lines, motion blur, manga style, anime eyes, exaggerated expressions,
flat colors, digital smoothness, neon colors, modern urban settings,
superhero proportions, chibi, cartoon exaggeration, halftone dots,
high saturation, fantasy elements, sci-fi technology
```
