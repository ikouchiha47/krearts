# STYLE: Fantasy RPG

## Overview
Epic fantasy illustration style inspired by Dungeons & Dragons, Magic: The Gathering, and classic fantasy art. Features detailed character designs, magical effects, mythical creatures, and richly imagined worlds. Combines realism with fantastical elements.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Line Work**: Detailed rendering with painterly qualities
- **Anatomy**: Heroic proportions, diverse fantasy races
- **Expressions**: Dramatic, emotionally charged
- **Panel Density**: 4-6 panels, emphasis on splash moments
- **Colors**: Rich, dramatic with magical lighting effects
- **Composition**: Epic scale, dramatic poses, magical effects

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: 4-6 panels with varied sizes
- **Dialogue Heavy**: Medium shots with character detail
- **Action Scenes**: 2-4 large dynamic panels
- **Establishing Shots**: Wide shots of castles, dungeons, landscapes
- **Splash Pages**: Common for dragons, magic, reveals

## Character Design References
- Detailed armor with fantasy embellishments
- Class-specific designs (warrior, mage, rogue, cleric)
- Fantasy race variations (elves, dwarves, orcs)
- Magical items and weapons with glow effects
- Cloaks, robes, and flowing fabric

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Hero vs monster confrontations
- Party group shots
- Magical spell casting moments
- Dungeon/cave exploration
- Castle/city establishing shots

## Color Palette References
- **Magic**: Blue arcane, green nature, red fire, purple dark
- **Metals**: Gold, silver, bronze, enchanted glow
- **Environments**: Forest greens, dungeon greys, castle stones
- **Evil**: Dark purples, sickly greens, blood reds
- **Divine**: Golden light, white radiance

## 0. Scope & Intent
Epic fantasy illustration style inspired by tabletop RPGs and collectible card games. Designed for rich, detailed storytelling with magical elements, mythical creatures, and heroic characters. Suitable for fantasy graphic novels and game-related comics.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
Detailed painterly illustration with dramatic lighting, magical effects, and epic scale. Combines realistic rendering with fantastical subject matter.

### Mood & Tone
- Epic
- Heroic
- Magical
- Adventurous

### Panel Density
4-6 panels per page, with frequent splash pages for dramatic moments

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Detailed but not overly tight
- Painterly quality in rendering
- Variable weight for depth
- Clean edges on important elements

### Shading
- Dramatic lighting from magical sources
- Rich shadows with color
- Rim lighting for drama
- Atmospheric perspective for depth

### Texture
- Detailed armor and weapon surfaces
- Fabric folds and material differentiation
- Environmental textures (stone, wood, foliage)
- Magical effects with glow and particles

### Avoid
- Flat, simple coloring
- Minimal backgrounds
- Modern/sci-fi elements
- Overly cartoony proportions
- Mundane lighting

## 3. Canonical Panel Examples

### Establishing
- Castle silhouettes against dramatic skies
- Dungeon entrances with torchlight
- Fantasy landscapes with impossible geography
- Magical realms with otherworldly elements

### Character
- Heroic poses with detailed equipment
- Class-specific action moments
- Party group compositions
- Villain reveals with dramatic lighting

### Action
- Spell casting with visible magic effects
- Combat with dynamic poses
- Monster encounters at scale
- Magical explosions and impacts

### Detail
- Weapon and armor close-ups
- Magical item reveals
- Creature design details
- Environmental storytelling elements

## 4. Character Design

### Anatomy
- Heroic proportions (idealized but believable)
- Fantasy race variations
- Age and experience shown
- Battle scars and wear

### Faces
- Expressive and dramatic
- Race-specific features
- Emotional intensity
- Detailed eyes

### Clothing
- Class-appropriate gear
- Fantasy embellishments
- Practical but stylized
- Material differentiation

### Pose Language
- Heroic stances
- Combat-ready positions
- Magical gestures
- Character personality in pose

## 5. Layout & Composition

- Varied panel sizes for pacing
- Splash pages for epic moments
- Clear action flow
- Environmental framing
- Dramatic angles for scale
- Magical effects crossing panels

## 6. Color System

- **Arcane Magic**: Blues, purples, silver sparkles
- **Nature Magic**: Greens, browns, golden light
- **Fire Magic**: Oranges, reds, yellow cores
- **Dark Magic**: Purples, sickly greens, black smoke
- **Divine Magic**: Golds, whites, radiant beams
- **Metals**: Realistic with magical enhancement
- **Environments**: Rich, saturated, atmospheric

```
Epic fantasy RPG comic illustration composed as a multi-panel page.
Detailed painterly rendering with dramatic magical lighting.
Rich saturated colors with atmospheric depth.
Professional fantasy art quality, D&D and MTG influence.

Panel composition emphasizes epic scale and dramatic moments.
Varied panel sizes with splash pages for major reveals.
Dynamic action sequences and heroic character poses.
Environmental storytelling with detailed fantasy settings.

Characters drawn with heroic proportions and detailed equipment.
Class-specific designs: warriors in plate armor, mages in robes,
rogues in leather, clerics with holy symbols.
Fantasy races rendered with distinctive features.

Magical effects visible and dramatic: glowing runes, energy beams,
elemental manifestations, divine light, dark corruption.
Creatures and monsters rendered with detail and menace.
Dragons, demons, undead, and mythical beasts.

Environments are richly imagined fantasy settings:
castles, dungeons, enchanted forests, volcanic lairs,
celestial realms, underground caverns.
Dramatic lighting from torches, magic, and otherworldly sources.

Professional fantasy illustration aesthetic.
Suitable for high-quality graphic novel reproduction.
```

### Negative Prompt
```
modern technology, sci-fi elements, contemporary clothing,
flat simple coloring, minimal backgrounds, mundane lighting,
chibi style, anime proportions, cartoon exaggeration,
photorealistic rendering, muted colors, urban settings
```

## 8. Sound Effects & Onomatopoeia

### Combat Sounds
- `CLANG` - Sword on sword
- `SLASH` - Blade cutting
- `THUNK` - Arrow impact
- `CRASH` - Shield block
- `CRUNCH` - Armor breaking

### Magic Sounds
- `FWOOOM` - Fire spell
- `CRACKLE` - Lightning
- `SHIMMER` - Enchantment
- `WHOOSH` - Wind magic
- `RUMBLE` - Earth magic

### Creature Sounds
- `ROAR` - Dragon/beast
- `SCREECH` - Flying creature
- `GROWL` - Monster threat
- `HISS` - Serpent/undead
- `STOMP` - Giant footsteps

### Environmental Sounds
- `CREAK` - Dungeon doors
- `DRIP` - Cave water
- `ECHO` - Vast chambers
- `RUSTLE` - Forest movement
- `HOWL` - Wind/wolves

### Lettering Style
- Medieval/fantasy fonts for effects
- Parchment-style speech bubbles
- Narrative boxes as quest log style
- Arcane scripts for magic words
- Runic or elvish for ancient languages
