# STYLE: Noir

## Overview
Classic film noir aesthetic adapted for comics. High contrast black and white imagery, dramatic shadows, rain-soaked urban environments, and morally ambiguous narratives. Influenced by Sin City, classic detective comics, and 1940s cinema.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Line Work**: Bold, confident strokes with high contrast edges
- **Anatomy**: Strong silhouettes readable in pure black
- **Expressions**: Hard-boiled, cynical, often half in shadow
- **Panel Density**: 5-7 panels per page, heavy use of silhouettes
- **Colors**: Extreme black and white (selective red only)
- **Composition**: Dramatic shadow casting, venetian blind patterns

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: 5-7 panels with silhouette emphasis
- **Dialogue Heavy**: Close-ups with dramatic lighting
- **Action Scenes**: Muzzle flash as white burst, silhouettes in combat
- **Establishing Shots**: Rain-slicked streets, urban skyline silhouettes
- **Detail Panels**: Gun in shadow, cigarette smoke, rain on window

## Character Design References
- Strong silhouettes readable in pure black
- Fedoras and trench coats (period 1940s fashion)
- Femme fatale glamour with dangerous elegance
- Weathered features, cynical eyes
- World-weary stance, confrontational angles

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Single harsh light source per panel
- Venetian blind shadows across faces
- Long shadows stretching dramatically
- Reflections in puddles and wet pavement
- Smoke as compositional element

## Color Palette References
- **Primary**: Pure black (#000000), Pure white (#FFFFFF)
- **Grey**: Minimal, sparingly used for specific depth
- **Selective Color**: Blood red (#8B0000), Lipstick red (#C41E3A)
- **Application**: 80%+ should be black or white
- **Rule**: Red ONLY for blood or lips, never full color

## 0. Scope & Intent
Classic film noir aesthetic adapted for comics. High contrast black and white imagery, dramatic shadows, rain-soaked urban environments, and morally ambiguous narratives. Influenced by Sin City, classic detective comics, and 1940s cinema.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
Extreme black and white contrast with dramatic shadow casting, emphasizing mood, mystery, and moral ambiguity through stark visual language.

### Mood & Tone
- Cynical
- Fatalistic
- Mysterious
- Morally grey

### Panel Density
5-7 panels per page, heavy use of silhouettes and shadow

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Bold, confident strokes
- High contrast edges
- Minimal mid-tones
- Silhouette emphasis

### Shading
- Extreme black and white
- Minimal grey tones
- Dramatic shadow casting
- Unrealistic shadows for mood

### Texture
- Rain streaks
- Smoke wisps
- Wet surface reflections
- Venetian blind patterns

### Avoid
- Color (except selective red)
- Soft gradients
- Even lighting
- Bright environments
- Happy/optimistic mood

- Pure black (#000000)

- Minimal grey (sparingly)

## 7. Prompt Specification (Authoritative)

### Base Prompt
```
Film noir comic illustration composed as a multi-panel page.
Extreme high contrast black and white artwork.
Bold solid blacks and stark white highlights, minimal grey tones.
Dramatic shadow casting for mood, not realism.

Panel composition emphasizes silhouettes and negative space.
Single harsh light sources creating venetian blind shadow patterns.
Rain-slicked urban environments at night.
Fog and smoke as compositional elements.

Characters as strong silhouettes readable in pure black.
Half-faces in shadow, cigarette smoke curling.
Fedoras, trench coats, period 1940s fashion.
Femme fatale glamour with dangerous elegance.
Hard-boiled detective archetypes.

Urban noir settings: rain-soaked streets, smoky bars, dark alleys.
Neon signs as rare light sources.
Reflections in puddles and wet pavement.
Oppressive urban atmosphere.

If any color used, ONLY selective red for blood or lipstick.
Otherwise pure black and white.
Sin City influence, classic noir aesthetic.
Printed comic suitable for noir graphic novel.
```

### Negative Prompt
```
full color, soft gradients, bright lighting, daytime scenes,
happy mood, soft shading, anime style, cartoon exaggeration,
natural landscapes, fantasy elements, sci-fi technology,
even lighting, low contrast, pastel colors, watercolor
```

## 8. Sound Effects & Onomatopoeia

### Violence Sounds
- `BLAM` - Gunshot (stark white on black)
- `CRACK` - Punch/impact
- `THUD` - Body falling
- `SPLAT` - Blood impact
- `SNAP` - Bone breaking

### Environmental Sounds
- `PATTER` - Rain on windows
- `CREAK` - Door opening
- `DRIP` - Water in darkness
- `CLICK` - Gun cocking
- `SCREECH` - Tires on wet pavement

### Atmospheric Sounds
- `TICK TICK` - Clock in silence
- `RING` - Phone in empty room
- `SILENCE` - Emphasized quiet (text)

### Lettering Style
- Bold, stark white on black (or reverse)
- Heavy black borders on bubbles
- First-person narration boxes
- Classic or typewriter fonts
- Minimal, impactful placement

## 9. Visual Effect Conventions

### Shadow Techniques
- **Venetian Blinds**: Horizontal stripe shadows across faces
- **Silhouettes**: Characters as pure black shapes
- **Spotlighting**: Single harsh light source
- **Long Shadows**: Stretching dramatically across panels

### Atmospheric Effects
- **Rain Streaks**: Diagonal white lines on black
- **Smoke Wisps**: Curling from cigarettes, guns
- **Fog**: Obscuring backgrounds
- **Reflections**: In puddles, windows, mirrors

### Narrative Conventions
- First-person cynical narration
- Flashback structure
- Moral ambiguity
- Doomed romance
- Betrayal reveals

## 10. QA Checklist

- [ ] Black and white dominates (80%+)
- [ ] Shadows are dramatic, not realistic
- [ ] Silhouettes read clearly
- [ ] Rain or wet surfaces present
- [ ] Mood is cynical/mysterious
- [ ] If color used, ONLY red accent
- [ ] Period-appropriate costume/setting
- [ ] Single light source per panel
