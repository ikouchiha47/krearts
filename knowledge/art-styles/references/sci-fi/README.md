# STYLE: Sci-Fi (Clean Futuristic)

## Overview
Optimistic science fiction aesthetic featuring sleek technology, clean environments, and advanced civilizations. Influenced by Star Trek, The Expanse, and classic sci-fi illustration. Emphasizes wonder, exploration, and technological achievement.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Line Work**: Clean, precise lines with technical quality
- **Anatomy**: Realistic proportions in functional uniforms
- **Expressions**: Professional, composed, exploratory wonder
- **Panel Density**: 5-6 panels, clear storytelling
- **Colors**: Clean whites, blues, with accent colors
- **Composition**: Emphasizing scale and technology

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: 5-6 panels with clear compositions
- **Dialogue Heavy**: Clean, well-lit character moments
- **Action Scenes**: Dynamic but controlled compositions
- **Establishing Shots**: Wide shots of ships, stations, planets
- **Tech Focus**: Detailed panels of interfaces and equipment

## Character Design References
- Functional uniforms and jumpsuits
- Clean, professional appearance
- Diverse crew representations
- Subtle tech integration (communicators, tools)
- Practical space-appropriate clothing

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Ships against planet/star backdrops
- Interior station/ship environments
- Holographic interface interactions
- EVA (spacewalk) scenes
- Bridge/command center views

## Color Palette References
- **Starship Interiors**: Clean whites, soft blues, accent lighting
- **Space**: Deep blacks with star fields, nebula colors
- **Planets**: Varied based on type (Earth-like, gas giant, etc.)
- **Technology**: Blue holographics, green/amber displays
- **Uniforms**: Color-coded by department/rank

## 0. Scope & Intent
Optimistic science fiction aesthetic emphasizing wonder, exploration, and technological achievement. Influenced by Star Trek, The Expanse, and classic sci-fi illustration. Designed for stories of space exploration, first contact, and human advancement.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
Clean, bright science fiction with sleek technology, functional design, and optimistic future vision. Professional illustration quality with technical accuracy.

### Mood & Tone
- Optimistic
- Wonder-filled
- Professional
- Exploratory

### Panel Density
5-6 panels per page, clear and efficient storytelling

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Clean, precise lines
- Technical illustration quality
- Consistent weight
- Sharp edges on technology

### Shading
- Bright, even lighting
- Soft shadows
- Rim lighting for drama
- Reflective surfaces on tech

### Texture
- Smooth technological surfaces
- Fabric uniforms with subtle folds
- Metallic reflections
- Holographic transparency

### Avoid
- Gritty, dirty aesthetics
- Dystopian decay
- Dark, oppressive lighting
- Cyberpunk neon excess
- Fantasy elements

## 8. Sound Effects & Onomatopoeia

### Technology Sounds
- `BEEP` - Computer/interface
- `WHOOSH` - Doors opening
- `HUM` - Engine/power systems
- `PING` - Sensor/notification
- `CHIRP` - Communicator

### Spacecraft Sounds
- `THRUM` - Engine power
- `CLANK` - Docking
- `HISS` - Airlock
- `RUMBLE` - Thrust
- `WHINE` - Systems powering up

### Weapon/Combat Sounds
- `PEW` - Energy weapon
- `ZAP` - Stun setting
- `FWOOSH` - Torpedo launch
- `BOOM` - Impact/explosion
- `CRACKLE` - Shield hit

### Environmental Sounds
- `STATIC` - Communication interference
- `ALARM` - Red alert
- `ECHO` - Large spaces
- `SILENCE` - Space vacuum (emphasized)

### Lettering Style
- Clean, technical fonts
- Modern speech bubble design
- Log entry captions
- In-world display text
- Alien language scripts (stylized)
