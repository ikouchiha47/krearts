# Sci-Fi Reference Images

Add 2-3 reference images here to influence the sci-fi art style.

## Recommended Images:
- Futuristic technology and spacecraft
- Clean, sleek sci-fi environments
- Advanced tech interfaces (non-neon)
- Space stations and orbital structures
- Scientific equipment and labs
- Optimistic future aesthetics

## Supported Formats:
- JPG/JPEG
- PNG
- WebP

## Usage:
These images will be automatically loaded when generating pages with
the "sci-fi" art style.

## Note:
Sci-fi differs from cyberpunk in tone and aesthetic:
- Sci-fi: Clean, optimistic, advanced technology
- Cyberpunk: Gritty, dystopian, neon-drenched, high-tech/low-life
