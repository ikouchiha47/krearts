# STYLE: Akira-Like Cinematic Manga

## Overview
Late-1980s Japanese cinematic manga realism focused on motion, machinery, and urban tension. Designed for black-and-white print aesthetics. Influenced by Katsuhiro Otomo's groundbreaking work in the Akira manga series.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Line Work**: Varied weight with heavy blacks, sharp mechanical edges
- **Anatomy**: Realistic, grounded proportions (NOT typical anime exaggeration)
- **Expressions**: Restrained, intense, weathered faces with deep eye shadows
- **Panel Density**: 6-8 panels per page, dynamic non-uniform layouts
- **Colors**: Primarily monochrome (black and white), selective color accents
- **Composition**: Extreme perspective, aggressive foreshortening, speed lines

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: 6-8 panels with thin gutters for rapid rhythm
- **Dialogue Heavy**: Dense panels with overlapping during chase sequences
- **Action Scenes**: Diagonal borders, widescreen cinematic panels
- **Establishing Shots**: Wide horizontal panels, low angle shots
- **Reading Direction**: Right-to-left (traditional manga)

## Character Design References
- Realistic, grounded proportions with detailed musculature
- Sharp noses, defined features, deep eye shadows
- Functional, worn clothing (biker gang aesthetic)
- Jackets, helmets, gloves, boots (period-appropriate 1980s)
- Forward-leaning, aggressive motion poses

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Extreme perspective with strong vanishing points
- Diagonal compositions for speed and tension
- Speed lines dominating frame during action
- Low angle shots of urban infrastructure
- Overlapping panels during chase sequences

## Color Palette References
- **Primary**: Monochrome (black and white)
- **Selective Color**: Blood red, electric blue (when used)
- **Neo-Tokyo Night**: Neon accents only for emphasis
- **Explosions**: Orange/white hot cores

## 0. Scope & Intent
Late-1980s Japanese cinematic manga realism focused on motion, machinery, and urban tension. Designed for black-and-white print aesthetics. Influenced by Katsuhiro Otomo's groundbreaking work.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
High-contrast ink manga emphasizing perspective, speed, and industrial scale.

### Mood & Tone
- Aggressive
- Tense
- Mechanical
- Dystopian

### Panel Density
6-8 panels per page, dynamic non-uniform layouts

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Varied weight, heavy blacks
- Sharp mechanical edges
- Dense crosshatching for texture
- Clean but aggressive ink lines

### Shading
- Solid blacks + speed lines
- No gradients
- High contrast only

### Texture
- Clean print manga aesthetic
- No paper noise
- Mechanical detail obsession

### Avoid
- Color (unless selective accent)
- Painterly shading
- Anime facial stylization
- Soft gradients
- Motion blur effects

## 3. Canonical Panel Examples

### Establishing
- Wide horizontal panels
- Low angle shots
- Urban infrastructure (highways, towers)
- Neo-Tokyo cityscape

### Action
- Diagonal composition
- Extreme perspective
- Speed lines dominate frame
- Aggressive foreshortening

### Detail
- Wheels, engines, exhaust
- Helmets, mechanical stress
- Tight crop, high contrast
- Forward thrust emphasis

### Impact
- Radial composition for explosions
- Debris and shockwave detail
- Psychic energy visualization

## 7. Prompt Specification (Authoritative)

### Base Prompt
```
Black-and-white cinematic manga illustration composed as a multi-panel comic page.
High-contrast ink artwork with bold solid blacks, sharp white highlights,
and dense hand-drawn speed lines. No color, no gradients, no soft shading.

Dynamic panel composition featuring a mix of wide horizontal establishing panels
and tight action inserts. Extreme perspective with low-angle shots, aggressive
foreshortening, strong vanishing points, and diagonal compositions to convey speed
and momentum. Motion is expressed through ink speed lines and perspective distortion,
not blur effects.

Futuristic motorcycles racing at high speed through an industrial urban environment.
Detailed mechanical rendering of wheels, suspension, engines, exhaust trails,
and headlights. Close-up panels emphasize spinning tires, mechanical stress,
and forward thrust.

Characters drawn with realistic anatomy and grounded proportions.
Sharp facial features, restrained expressions, deep shadows around the eyes.
Functional clothing such as jackets, helmets, gloves, and boots.
Body language is forward-leaning, tense, and aggressive, always angled into motion.

Urban setting dominated by concrete structures, highways, tunnels, bridges,
and industrial architecture. Night-time atmosphere with harsh lighting contrasts.
Gritty, oppressive city scale with no decorative elements.

Printed late-1980s Japanese manga aesthetic.
Clean but aggressive ink linework.
Editorial comic page layout suitable for physical print.
```

### Negative Prompt
```
color, grayscale gradients, soft shading, painterly style, watercolor,
digital painting, anime facial proportions, chibi, exaggerated eyes,
3D render, CGI, smooth surfaces, neon glow, lens blur, motion blur,
modern webtoon style, pastel tones, vector art
```

## 8. Sound Effects & Onomatopoeia

### Impact/Explosion (Japanese)
- `ドォォォン` (DOOON) - Deep explosion
- `ズガァァン` (ZUGAAAN) - Massive impact
- `バキィ` (BAKII) - Breaking/cracking
- `ドカァン` (DOKAAN) - Explosion burst

### Motion/Speed (Japanese)
- `ギュイィィン` (GYUIIIN) - High-speed movement
- `ブォォォ` (BUOOO) - Engine roar
- `キィィィ` (KIIII) - Tire screech
- `ヒュウウウ` (HYUUUU) - Whooshing air

### Psychic/Energy
- `ビリビリ` (BIRIBIRI) - Electric crackling
- `ゴゴゴゴ` (GOGOGO) - Ominous rumbling/menacing aura
- `ドクン` (DOKUN) - Heartbeat/pulse

### English Equivalents
- Explosions: `KRAKA-DOOM` `THOOM` `BRAKKA-BOOM`
- Impacts: `KRAK` `SMASH` `WHAM`
- Speed: `VRRRRM` `SKREEE` `WHOOOOSH`

### Lettering Style
- Sound effects integrated INTO artwork
- Follow motion line direction
- Large, dramatic, part of composition
- Sharp-edged speech bubbles for intensity

## 9. QA Checklist

- [ ] Motion reads instantly from composition
- [ ] Blacks dominate the page
- [ ] No softness or gradients
- [ ] Speed lines are hand-drawn style
- [ ] Mechanical detail is obsessive
- [ ] Faces are realistic, not anime
- [ ] Perspective is extreme and aggressive
- [ ] Urban scale feels oppressive
