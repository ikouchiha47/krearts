# STYLE: Cyberpunk Noir

## Overview
Dystopian science fiction aesthetic combining high technology with urban decay. Neon-drenched cityscapes, rain-slicked streets, cybernetic enhancements. "High tech, low life" philosophy. Influenced by Blade Runner, Ghost in the Shell, Neuromancer.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Line Work**: Sharp, clean lines with angular silhouettes and hard edges
- **Anatomy**: Realistic but stylized with cybernetic integration visible
- **Expressions**: Minimal, controlled, partially shadowed eyes
- **Panel Density**: 4-6 panels per page, cinematic widescreen compositions
- **Colors**: Dark base with neon accent colors (pink, cyan, purple)
- **Composition**: Vertical panels for city height, layered depth

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: 4-6 panels with asymmetrical layouts
- **Dialogue Heavy**: Split lighting character shots
- **Action Scenes**: Muzzle flash highlights, holographic HUD overlays
- **Establishing Shots**: Vertical panels showing towering megastructures
- **Detail Panels**: Cybernetic implants, holographic interfaces

## Character Design References
- Angular facial features, sharp jawlines
- Cybernetic implants and augmentations
- Futuristic techwear with layered synthetic fabrics
- Armor panels, visors, cables, glowing interfaces
- Controlled, confrontational body language

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Asymmetrical panel layouts with varied sizes
- Vertical panels emphasizing city height
- Low-angle perspectives for power
- Layered depth (foreground tech, background city)
- HUD overlay panels integrated into art

## Color Palette References
- **Base**: Deep blacks, charcoal (#0a0a1a, #1a1a2e)
- **Neon Accents**: Hot pink (#FF1493), Electric cyan (#00FFFF), Purple (#9400D3)
- **Highlights**: White neon glow, yellow warnings
- **Rain/Wet**: Multiplied reflections of all colors
- **Skin**: Lit by colored neon, unnatural hues

## 0. Scope & Intent
Dystopian science fiction aesthetic combining high technology with urban decay. Neon-drenched cityscapes, rain-slicked streets, cybernetic enhancements. "High tech, low life" philosophy. Influenced by Blade Runner, Ghost in the Shell, Neuromancer.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
High-contrast noir with neon accents, emphasizing urban density, technological integration, and atmospheric tension.

### Mood & Tone
- Oppressive
- Noir-influenced
- Technologically saturated
- Morally ambiguous

### Panel Density
4-6 panels per page, cinematic widescreen compositions

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Sharp, clean lines
- Angular silhouettes
- Hard edges
- Stylized realism

### Shading
- Strong solid blacks
- Controlled highlights
- Hard shadows from artificial light
- Rim lighting emphasis

### Texture
- Rain on surfaces
- Reflective wet streets
- Holographic interference
- Industrial grime

### Avoid
- Soft painterly rendering
- Natural landscapes
- Medieval/fantasy elements
- Pastel colors
- Flat lighting

## 7. Prompt Specification (Authoritative)

### Base Prompt
```
High-contrast cyberpunk comic illustration composed as a multi-panel page.
Sharp, clean linework with angular silhouettes and hard edges.
Strong use of solid blacks and controlled highlights.
Stylized realism with graphic clarity, not painterly rendering.

Dynamic panel layouts with asymmetrical compositions and varied panel sizes.
Vertical panels emphasize city height and density.
Camera angles favor low-angle views, oblique perspectives,
and layered depth to convey scale and surveillance.

Characters drawn with realistic but stylized anatomy.
Angular facial features, sharp jawlines, and minimal expressions.
Eyes often partially shadowed or lit by artificial light.
Body language is controlled, confident, and confrontational.

Costumes feature futuristic techwear, layered fabrics, synthetic materials,
armor panels, cybernetic implants, visors, cables, and glowing interfaces.
Props include weapons, devices, holographic screens, and HUD elements.

Urban environments are dense and oppressive:
towering megastructures, neon signage, rain-soaked streets,
industrial interiors, cables, pipes, vents, and screens.
Lighting is artificial and directional, creating hard shadows and rim light.

Color palette is dark and limited, dominated by blacks and deep shadows,
with neon accent colors used sparingly for highlights and signage.
Strong contrast between darkness and light sources.

Printed comic aesthetic with digital-era subject matter.
Crisp illustration suitable for graphic novel reproduction.
```

### Negative Prompt
```
pastel colors, watercolor, painterly softness, fantasy armor,
medieval elements, natural landscapes, western themes,
anime facial proportions, chibi, cartoon exaggeration,
low-contrast lighting, flat color fills, bright daylight,
clean utopian environments, soft gradients
```

## 8. Sound Effects & Onomatopoeia

### Technology
- `BZZZT` - Electronic interference
- `WHIRRRR` - Servo motors
- `PING` - Interface notification
- `CRACKLE` - Hologram static
- `HUMMM` - Power systems

### Weapons/Combat
- `PEW PEW` - Energy weapons
- `BRAKKKA` - Automatic gunfire
- `SIZZLE` - Laser/plasma
- `CLANK` - Metal cybernetics
- `THOOM` - Heavy impact

### Environment
- `PATTER` - Rain on metal
- `WHOOSH` - Flying vehicles
- `DRIP` - Water in alleys
- `RUMBLE` - City ambience

### Lettering Style
- Tech-styled fonts with glow
- Angular speech bubbles
- Monospace for internal monologue
- HUD text as part of art
- Digital artifacts on stressed text

## 9. QA Checklist

- [ ] Rain/wet surfaces present
- [ ] Vertical scale emphasized
- [ ] Hard shadows from artificial light
