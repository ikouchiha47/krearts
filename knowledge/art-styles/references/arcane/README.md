# STYLE: Arcane Painterly Animation

## Overview
Painterly animated style from Netflix's "Arcane" (2021) by Fortiche Productions. Traditional oil painting aesthetics combined with 3D animation. Visible brushwork, dramatic cinematic lighting, rich saturated colors. Revolutionary blend of fine art and animation.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Line Work**: Soft edges defined by color/value, not hard outlines
- **Anatomy**: Realistic but stylized with unique silhouettes per character
- **Expressions**: Subtle emotional range with psychological depth
- **Panel Density**: 3-5 panels per page, emphasis on mood over action density
- **Colors**: Rich saturated colors with dramatic cinematic lighting
- **Composition**: Cinematic framing with atmospheric perspective

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: 3-5 panels with emphasis on mood and expression
- **Dialogue Heavy**: Intimate character close-ups
- **Action Scenes**: Painterly motion through color contrast, not speed lines
- **Establishing Shots**: Wide atmospheric cityscapes with dramatic sky
- **Emotional Moments**: Large panels with rich detail

## Character Design References
- Painterly skin rendering with visible brushstrokes
- Detailed eyes carrying narrative weight
- Unique silhouettes and color palettes per character
- Texture and wear detail on clothing
- Emotionally driven body language

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Wide cinematic establishing shots
- Intimate emotional close-ups
- Dramatic rim lighting separating characters from backgrounds
- Atmospheric perspective creating depth through haze
- Rule of thirds and golden ratio framing

- **Zaun**: Green smog, purple shadows, neon accents, decay

## 0. Scope & Intent
Painterly animated style from Netflix's "Arcane" (2021) by Fortiche Productions. Traditional oil painting aesthetics combined with 3D animation. Visible brushwork, dramatic cinematic lighting, rich saturated colors. Revolutionary blend of fine art and animation.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
Fine art painting brought to animation. Visible brushstrokes throughout, dramatic lighting, rich color grading, and emotional depth through painterly rendering.

### Mood & Tone
- Cinematic
- Emotional
- Atmospheric
- Painterly

### Panel Density
3-5 panels per page, emphasis on mood, expression, and atmosphere over action density

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Soft edges, not hard outlines
- Form defined by color/value
- Brushstroke direction guides eye
- Painterly, not graphic

### Shading
- Visible brushwork throughout
- Rich color in shadows
- Dramatic rim lighting
- Atmospheric perspective

### Texture
- Canvas/paint texture overlay
- Visible brush strokes on all surfaces
- Impasto effects on highlights
- Soft edges between colors

### Avoid
- Hard black outlines
- Flat color fills
- Clean digital rendering
- Anime-style simplification
- Halftone or print effects

## 9. Visual Effect Conventions

### Painterly Techniques
- **Brushwork**: Visible in all surfaces
- **Impasto**: Thick paint on highlights
- **Glazing**: Transparent color layers
- **Scumbling**: Broken color for texture

### Lighting Effects
- **Rim Light**: Characters outlined with light
- **Atmospheric Haze**: Depth through fog/smog
- **Color Grading**: Scene-specific palettes
- **Volumetric Light**: Beams through particles

### Emotional Visual Language
- **Jinx Chaos**: Swirling colors, unstable compositions
- **Vi Determination**: Warm, grounded, forward momentum
- **Tragedy**: Desaturated with single color accent
- **Hope**: Golden light breaking through darkness
