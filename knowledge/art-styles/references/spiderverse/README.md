# STYLE: Spider-Verse Animated Comic

## Overview
Revolutionary animated style from "Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse" (2018). Living comic book aesthetic combining halftone dots, chromatic aberration, and variable frame rates. Designed for hyper-stylized, kinetic visual storytelling.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Line Work**: Bold black outlines with hand-drawn quality, variable thickness
- **Anatomy**: Exaggerated for dynamic poses, stylized proportions
- **Expressions**: Bold, graphic, expressive but stylized
- **Panel Density**: 3-5 panels per page, large dynamic compositions
- **Colors**: Hyper-saturated primaries with chromatic aberration
- **Composition**: Extreme angles, Dutch tilts, 3D panel borders

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: 3-5 panels with integrated effects
- **Dialogue Heavy**: Character-focused with halftone shading
- **Action Scenes**: Chromatic aberration intensified, smear frames
- **Establishing Shots**: Extreme angles, city from above/below
- **Impact Panels**: Radial halftone explosion, 3D sound effects

## Character Design References
- Bold outline emphasis with halftone skin shading
- Unique "dimension" styling per character
- Graphic costume designs with strong silhouettes
- Impossible angles and comic book poses
- Mid-action freeze frames

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Extreme camera angles with Dutch tilts
- Comic panels appearing in 3D space
- Text integrated into environment as physical objects
- Split-screen for parallel action
- Vertical emphasis for swinging

## Color Palette References
- **Miles Morales**: Red, black, electric blue/purple
- **Gwen Stacy**: Pink, white, cyan, watercolor washes
- **Noir**: Pure black and white
- **General**: Hyper-saturated primaries
- **Effects**: RGB separation on all edges (chromatic aberration)

## 0. Scope & Intent
Revolutionary animated style from "Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse" (2018). Living comic book aesthetic combining halftone dots, chromatic aberration, and variable frame rates. Designed for hyper-stylized, kinetic visual storytelling.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
Mixed-media comic animation with visible print artifacts, bold graphics, and dimensional effects. Comic book brought to life.

### Mood & Tone
- Kinetic
- Bold
- Youthful
- Dimensional

### Panel Density
3-5 panels per page, large dynamic compositions with integrated effects

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Bold black outlines
- Hand-drawn quality
- Variable thickness
- "Smeared" during motion

### Shading
- Halftone Ben-Day dots visible
- Especially in shadows
- Dot size varies with distance

### Texture
- Print comic artifacts
- Chromatic aberration on edges
- CMYK separation visible
- Paper/print texture overlay

### Avoid
- Smooth gradients
- Photorealistic rendering
- Clean digital edges
- Subtle color transitions
- Traditional animation smoothness

## 3. Canonical Panel Examples

### Establishing
- Extreme angles (Dutch tilt)
- City from above/below
- Comic panel borders in 3D space
- Bold graphic shapes

### Action
- Chromatic aberration intensified
- Smear frames for motion
- Speed lines as environment
- Panel borders breaking

### Impact
- Radial halftone explosion
- Sound effects as 3D objects
- Kirby dots for energy
- Color separation maximum

### Character
- Bold outline emphasis
- Halftone skin shading
- Unique "dimension" styling per character
- Expressive poses

## 4. Character Design

### Anatomy
- Exaggerated for dynamic poses
- Stylized proportions
- Readable silhouettes

### Faces
- Bold features
- Expressive but graphic
- Outline defines form
- Halftone shading on skin

### Clothing
- Graphic patterns
- Bold costume colors
- Outline variation for depth
- Each character has unique style

### Pose Language
- Extreme dynamism
- Impossible angles
- Comic book poses
- Mid-action freeze frames

## 5. Layout & Composition

- Extreme camera angles
- Dutch tilts frequent
- Comic panels appear in 3D space

## 6. Color System

- **Miles Morales**: Red, black, electric blue/purple

## 7. Prompt Specification (Authoritative)

### Base Prompt
```
Spider-Verse animated comic style illustration composed as a dynamic panel page.
Bold black outlines with hand-drawn quality and variable thickness.
Visible halftone Ben-Day dot shading, especially in shadows and mid-tones.
Chromatic aberration RGB color separation on edges and during motion.

Extreme camera angles with Dutch tilts and impossible perspectives.
Comic panel borders appearing as 3D elements in the scene.
Sound effect text integrated into the environment as physical objects.
Dynamic compositions emphasizing vertical movement and swinging action.

Characters with exaggerated proportions for dynamic poses.
Bold graphic costume designs with strong silhouettes.
Halftone dot patterns on skin and clothing.
Each character maintains unique dimensional styling.

Urban environments rendered with graphic boldness.
New York cityscape from extreme angles.
Neon city lights against dark backgrounds.
Buildings as graphic shapes, not photorealistic.

Hyper-saturated color palette with bold primaries.
Strong contrast between light and shadow.
Chromatic aberration effects on all high-contrast edges.
Print comic texture overlay throughout.

Mixed 2D/3D aesthetic with visible stylization.
Animation frame suitable for motion but reads as comic art.
```

### Negative Prompt
```
smooth gradients, photorealistic rendering, subtle colors,

watercolor softness, 3D render without stylization
```

## 8. Sound Effects & Onomatopoeia

### Signature Sounds (3D Text in Environment)
- `THWIP` - Web shooting (iconic, red text)
- `BRAKKA-DOOM` - Explosion
- `KRAK` - Impact/punch
- `SMASH` - Breaking through
- `WHAM` - Heavy hit

### Motion Sounds
- `FWIP FWIP FWIP` - Rapid web-swinging
- `WHOOOOSH` - Fast movement
- `SKREEE` - Sliding/stopping
- `ZWIP` - Quick dodge

### Dimension/Glitch
- `GLITCH` - Dimension interference
- `BZZZT` - Electric/portal
- `KRACKLE` - Energy discharge

### Lettering Style
- **3D extruded text** as part of environment
- Colors match action (red impacts, blue webs)
- Sound effects ARE physical objects
- Bold comic fonts with dimension
- Traditional bubbles with modern flair

## 9. Visual Effect Conventions

- **Smear Frames**: Stretched character during fast motion
- **Held Frames**: Character freezes, background moves
- **Line Boil**: Hand-drawn lines subtly shift
- **Kirby Dots**: Classic energy dots for powers
- **Panel Borders**: Appear in 3D space
- **Chromatic Aberration**: RGB split on all edges

## 10. QA Checklist

- [ ] Halftone dots visible in shadows
- [ ] Chromatic aberration on edges
- [ ] Bold black outlines present
- [ ] Extreme camera angle used
- [ ] Colors are hyper-saturated
- [ ] Sound effects are 3D/integrated
- [ ] Comic book texture visible
- [ ] Dynamic pose, not static
