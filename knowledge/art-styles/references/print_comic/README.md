# STYLE: Print Comic (Classic Halftone)

## Overview
Classic print comic book aesthetic from the Golden and Silver Age of comics (1940s-1970s). Characterized by Ben-Day dots, limited color palettes due to printing limitations, bold outlines, and occasional color misregistration effects.

## Defining Characteristics
- **Line Work**: Bold black outlines, consistent weight
- **Anatomy**: Clear, readable silhouettes
- **Expressions**: Simplified facial features, clear emotions
- **Panel Density**: 6-9 panels, efficient storytelling
- **Colors**: Limited CMYK palette, flat color fills
- **Composition**: Clear, readable, functional layouts

## Panel Layout
- **Typical**: 6-9 panels, regular grid
- **Dialogue Heavy**: 8-9 smaller panels
- **Action Scenes**: 4-6 panels with larger key moments
- **Splash Pages**: Rare, reserved for covers and key moments
- **Gutters**: Consistent white space

## Character Design References
- Clear, readable silhouettes
- Bold costume colors for recognition
- Simplified facial features
- Consistent character models
- Period-appropriate fashion

## Layout/Composition Examples
- Regular grid layouts for clarity
- Clear panel-to-panel flow
- Minimal background detail
- Focus on character action
- Efficient visual storytelling

## Color Palette References
- **Primary**: Pure red, blue, yellow (limited mixing)
- **Secondary**: Green, orange, purple (dot patterns)
- **Skin Tones**: Limited range, dot-based shading
- **Backgrounds**: Often single flat color
- **Shadows**: Ben-Day dot gradients

## 0. Scope & Intent
Classic print comic book aesthetic replicating the look of Golden and Silver Age comics. Designed to capture the charm of limited printing technology including Ben-Day dots, color restrictions, and occasional misregistration.

## 1. Style Identity

### Summary
Vintage comic book printing aesthetic with visible halftone dots, bold outlines, and limited color palette. Nostalgic and authentic to the era.

### Mood & Tone
- Nostalgic
- Classic

- Accessible

### Panel Density
6-9 panels per page, efficient grid-based storytelling

## 2. Linework & Rendering Rules

### Linework
- Bold black outlines
- Consistent weight
- Clean, professional
- Clear silhouettes

### Shading
- Ben-Day halftone dots
- Visible dot patterns
- Limited tonal range
- No smooth gradients

### Texture
- Newsprint grain (optional)
- Halftone screens
- Flat color areas
- Ink bleed effects (subtle)

### Avoid
- Smooth digital gradients
- Complex color mixing
- Painterly rendering
- Modern coloring techniques
- High color counts

## 8. Sound Effects & Onomatopoeia

### Classic Impact Sounds
- `POW!` - The iconic punch
- `BAM!` - Heavy hit
- `WHAM!` - Powerful strike
- `SOCK!` - Punch
- `BOP!` - Light hit
- `CRASH!` - Breaking

### Explosion Sounds
- `BOOM!` - Standard explosion
- `KA-BOOM!` - Big explosion
- `BLAM!` - Gunshot/blast
- `KA-POW!` - Powerful impact

### Motion Sounds
- `ZOOM!` - Fast movement
- `WHOOSH!` - Air movement
- `ZIP!` - Quick action
- `SWISH!` - Swinging

### Environmental Sounds
- `RING!` - Phone/bell
- `KNOCK!` - Door
- `SPLASH!` - Water
- `CREAK!` - Door/floor

### Lettering Style
- Bold with exclamation marks
- Yellow, red, or white with black outline
- Perfect oval speech bubbles
- Cloud-shaped thought bubbles
- Yellow rectangular caption boxes
- Hand-lettered style, all caps
