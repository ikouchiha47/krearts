# Character Description Guidelines by Genre

## Purpose
This document provides genre-specific guidance for describing characters with visual representation in mind. All characters must be describable as visual entities for image generation.

---

## Sci-Fi / Cyberpunk Characters

### Human Characters
- Standard physical descriptions apply
- Include cybernetic enhancements visually: "cybernetic right arm with chrome plating and blue LED strips"
- Describe tech implants: "neural interface ports at temples", "glowing optical implants"

### AI / Holographic Characters
**IMPORTANT**: AI and interface characters MUST have a visual humanoid form.

**Good Examples:**
- "Holographic AI avatar with translucent blue glowing humanoid female form, ethereal digital appearance, circuit patterns visible within the projection, floating particles of light around the edges"
- "AI construct manifesting as a semi-transparent male figure composed of flowing cyan data streams, geometric patterns shifting across the surface, faint wireframe grid visible in low-light areas"
- "Synthetic intelligence appearing as an androgynous hologram with purple-tinted luminescence, facial features sharp and angular, body composed of intersecting light planes"

**Bad Examples (DO NOT USE):**
- ❌ "Disembodied voice interface"
- ❌ "Represented by glowing icons"
- ❌ "Abstract visualization"
- ❌ "Voice-only AI"

**Visual References:**
- Cortana (Halo series) - translucent blue holographic woman
- EDI (Mass Effect) - holographic avatar with defined humanoid form
- JARVIS visualization (MCU) - geometric humanoid light construct
- Joi (Blade Runner 2049) - solid-looking holographic woman

**Key Requirements:**
1. Must have humanoid shape (head, torso, limbs)
2. Must have facial features (eyes, mouth, expression capability)
3. Can be translucent/glowing but must be clearly person-shaped
4. Include specific color (blue, cyan, purple, etc.)
5. Describe texture (light-based, data streams, geometric patterns)

### Robot Characters
- Describe as mechanical humanoids with visible joints, plating, sensors
- Include material: "brushed titanium chassis", "carbon fiber limbs"
- Specify optical sensors: "glowing red optical sensors", "blue LED eye array"

---

## Fantasy Characters

### Magical Beings
- Must have physical form, even if ethereal
- "Spectral entity with translucent silvery form, flowing robes that merge with mist"
- NOT: "Formless magical presence"

### Non-Human Races
- Describe physical features clearly: "Elven features with pointed ears, luminous amber eyes"
- Include distinctive traits: "Dwarven build with braided beard, calloused hands"

---

## Horror Characters

### Supernatural Entities
- Must have describable form
- "Shadowy figure with elongated limbs, face obscured by darkness save for glowing white eyes"
- NOT: "Invisible presence" or "Formless dread"

---

## General Rules

1. **Every character needs a visual form** - even if supernatural, AI, or abstract
2. **Describe what the camera would see** - not what they "are" conceptually
3. **Include specific visual details**: colors, textures, proportions
4. **Think cinematically**: How would this appear on screen?
5. **Avoid abstract descriptions**: "represented by", "symbolized as", "manifests as ideas"

---

## Quick Reference

| Character Type | Visual Requirement | Example |
|----------------|-------------------|---------|
| Human | Standard description | "Tall, weathered face, sharp gray eyes" |
| AI/Hologram | Humanoid light form | "Translucent blue glowing humanoid form" |
| Robot | Mechanical humanoid | "Chrome chassis with articulated joints" |
| Ghost/Spirit | Ethereal humanoid | "Translucent silvery figure in flowing robes" |
| Alien | Physical form with traits | "Reptilian features, scaled green skin" |
| Demon | Monstrous humanoid | "Horned figure with crimson skin, clawed hands" |

**Remember**: If you can't describe what it looks like, it can't be generated as a character image.
