How It Works

From storyline to Instagram carousel in 4 steps

01

Start with a Storyline

Pick a genre (Detective Noir, Sci-Fi, Fantasy) or create your own. Define the world, conflict, and setting. No script needed, just the premise.

Detective Noir
"New Harbor City, 1952. Ex-cop Jack Malone investigates a missing person case that leads to murder, corruption, and betrayal."
02

Create Your Cast

Upload reference images or generate AI characters. Each character gets a "Character Card" with personality, backstory, quirks, and goals. This locks their appearance across all panels.

03

Let Agents Generate Drama

Our AI agents simulate your characters' behaviors, memories, and conflicts. They plan, reflect, and interact to create emergent narratives that surprise even you.

Veronica's Goal: "Protect my brother's secret. Don't let Jack find the ledger."
04

Publish & Spin-off

Export your 10-panel carousel to Instagram. Then hit "Spin-off" to create branching timelines. Ask "What if..." and generate infinite variations.
